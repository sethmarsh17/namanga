About Us

Who we are.

Namanga Hope Center is an organization established with the primary goal of supporting orphans and vulnerable children in Namanga, Kenya. Its mission is to ensure that these children have access to their fundamental rights, particularly in the areas of education and health. The center recognizes the critical importance of education in breaking the cycle of poverty and provides various programs to facilitate learning opportunities. This includes school sponsorships, provision of educational materials, and after-school tutoring sessions.
